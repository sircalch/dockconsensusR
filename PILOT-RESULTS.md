# Multitarget seed-consensus pilot result

The fixed seed-consensus protocol was executed on 2026-08-08. Each ligand was
observed in all three seed rankings for every target (method coverage = 1.0).
Pairwise Spearman agreement ranges were:

| Target | Pairwise Spearman rank agreement across three seeds |
|---|---:|
| ADA | 0.970–0.982 |
| AMPC | 0.985–0.998 |
| COMT | 0.930–0.975 |

These values describe rank agreement in the recorded 24-ligand pilot only.
They do not establish a correct consensus, molecular binding, biological
activity, efficacy, safety, or prospective screening performance. High global
rank correlation also does not by itself guarantee a stable identity at the
very top of a ranking.
